# Highlights

- If the naïve forecast and the lag share a key, a defect biases skill unpredictably
- At the cell level, global models beat persistence and ETS, ARIMA and Theta
- Exogenous features help depending on the period: with short history, not in 2026
- Stratified conformal: lower interval score than unstratified and ETS (except N7)
- The four 2×2 models and two benchmarks were frozen with a protocol for 2027
